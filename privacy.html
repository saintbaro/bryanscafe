Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

At Bryan’s Café, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you interact with our website, particularly through our enquiry form. We follow relevant privacy laws and organisational cybersecurity procedures to maintain the confidentiality and integrity of your information.

1. Information Collection

When you use our website, we may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through the enquiry form. This includes your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address. This information is collected only when necessary and for a clearly defined purpose. Collecting accurate and relevant information helps us respond effectively to your enquiries and provide better service to our customers.

We ensure that all data collection practices comply with privacy regulations and are limited to information that is necessary for communication and service improvement purposes.

2. Use of Information

The personal information we collect is used solely for responding to your enquiries, providing customer support, and improving our services. We may also use this information to enhance user experience and ensure that our website meets customer expectations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties unless required by law. All information is handled in accordance with organisational policies and ethical standards to ensure trust and transparency.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. These measures include secure data storage, restricted access, and regular monitoring of our systems.

Cybersecurity protocols are applied to safeguard sensitive information, as protecting data is essential in maintaining organisational credibility and preventing potential threats or breaches.

4. Data Retention

Your personal information will only be retained for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. Once the information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised in accordance with data protection standards.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. If you believe that any information we hold about you is incorrect or outdated, you may contact us to have it corrected.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through our official business contact details. We are committed to resolving any privacy-related concerns promptly and transparently.
